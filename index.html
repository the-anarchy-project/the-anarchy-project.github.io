The Anarchy Project

The goal of the project is to preserve the original industrial idea-antimusic. It is based on "machine sound",technical sounds, "electric" textures, the absence of melody or at least its minimization. I draw inspiration from such musical genres and styles as industrial, techno, drum and bass, dark/goa trance, EBM, dark ambient, death/black metal. This has a corresponding impact on the sound of the project.
